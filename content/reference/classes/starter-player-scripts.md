## Summary

`StarterPlayerScripts` is a container for most [LocalScripts](https://create.playvortex.io/reference/classes/localscript/) (since they can also be placed in [ReplicatedStorage](https://create.playvortex.io/reference/classes/replicated-storage/)). Similar to [ServerScriptService](https://create.playvortex.io/reference/classes/server-script-service/), which contains [Scripts](https://create.playvortex.io/reference/classes/script/).
